# FastLink 3PL Pharmacy Logistics — Database

A relational database designed and built for a third-party pharmacy logistics provider, delivering pharmaceutical products under GDP (Good Distribution Practice) cold-chain rules. NCI Higher Diploma in Data Analytics.

## What it does
Records customers, orders, shipments, suppliers, medicines and temperature readings, and enforces cold-chain compliance — flagging any reading outside a medicine's required range.

## Design & techniques
- Conceptual, logical and physical design from 13 business rules
- Entity Relationship Diagram (Crow's Foot notation, 9 entities)
- Normalised to Third Normal Form (3NF)
- SQL DDL (schema) and DML (data population)
- Reporting queries, including temperature-compliance checks
